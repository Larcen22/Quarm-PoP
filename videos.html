About these videos

This page collects community-made YouTube videos for the Planes of Power expansion, grouped by flag and boss in progression order. Click any thumbnail to open it on YouTube (opens in a new tab).

  • Videos come from many servers — TAKP, TLP, and various progression/private servers — so some mechanics, loot tables, and group sizes may differ slightly from this Quarm-based guide.
  • Quality and accuracy vary by uploader. Treat them as supplementary reference alongside the written guides on this site.
  • Thumbnails are loaded lazily; nothing plays until you click through to YouTube.
