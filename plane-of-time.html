Plane of Time

The five trials, the branches, the trash clear, the Lesser Gods, the Major Gods, and Quarm

Overview

The Plane of Time is the final zone. The raid consists of 6 phases and after each phase is completed you are given extra time to complete the raid. You start in Plane of Time A and when ready send your forces to the portals shown below; the raid begins when you click in.

  • Z — Zone in/out
  • A — Undead
  • B — Fire
  • C — Earth
  • D — Water
  • E — Air

The six phases, in order: Phase 1 — The Five Trials, Phase 2 — The Three Branches, Phase 3 — The Trash Clear, Phase 4 — The Lesser Gods, Phase 5 — The Major Gods, and Phase 6 — Quarm.

Phase 1 — The Five Trials

For the first phase there are 5 events that need to be completed. Each event can only have 18 people in them. You have 1 hour to complete all 5 to advance the script. Lockout for phase 1 is 12 hours.

Neimon of Air

Expansion: Planes of Power · Zone: Plane of Time · Level: 68

The Air Trial is a quick trial but requires some CC and/or aggro management. All mobs in this trial can be slowed. There is a total of 2 waves.

Wave 1

4x An Air Phoenix Noble spawn. They can be either rootable, mezzable, or neither. It can be different each time. The nobles hit for 900 and have nothing special.

Wave 2

4x Servitor of Xegony spawn along with the boss, Neimon of Air. All of these will spawn along the back wall where the door is. The 2 inner ones can be mezzed, the outer 2 can be rooted or off-tanked. The servitors hit for about 1,000 and are immune to mez but can be rooted. You can kill the mobs in Wave 2 whatever order is easiest for your raid. For us it is usually easiest to kill the Neimon of Air first, then kill the rooted/off-tanked birds, then the mezzed birds. You’ll want to dispel Neimon of Air as well (see his stat info below).

Terlok of Earth

Expansion: Planes of Power · Zone: Plane of Time · Level: 68

Everything in the Earth Trial is slowable. It can take a while, so bring a decent amount of DPS to this trial to get it done in a timely manner.

This trial starts off with 3x a pile of living rubble spawning. They cannot be mezzed or rooted so need to be tanked, and hit for almost 1,000. Each time you kill one, 2x to 4x a rock shaped assassin will spawn; they hit for 800 and can be mezzed and rooted. Kill them off then move onto the next pile of living rubble.

After the 6th rock shaped assassin dies (a total of 10 kills), Terlok of Earth spawns. Kill him to win the trial, then finish off any remaining trash mobs. He quads for 1,100 but has no tricks.

Kazrok of Fire

Expansion: Planes of Power · Zone: Plane of Time · Level: 68

Everything in this trial can be slowed. The Fire Trial has a few different waves.

Wave 1

It starts off with 3x a flame mephit (big mephits) spawning. Each time you kill one, 2x inferno mephit spawn (small mephits). Kill them before moving onto the next big mephit. The big mephits cannot be mezzed and can only sometimes be rooted. The small mephits can sometimes be mezzed or rooted.

Wave 2

Once all trash is dead the boss, Kazrok of Fire, will spawn. You’ll want to dispel his buff and get him slowed ASAP. He will spawn 2 additional adds at 75%, 50%, and 25%. A flame mephit is mezzable. An inferno mephit is rootable.

Anar of Water

Expansion: Planes of Power · Zone: Plane of Time · Level: 68

Water Trial is essentially one long wave. The boss, Anar of Water, spawns right away along with 2x a triloun of water adds. Anar of Water cannot be slowed, but the adds can. The trilouns hit 900ish and can be rooted.

The boss will also spawn another 2x a deepwater triloun mobs at roughly 90% and 45% HP. This means a total of 6 adds if you count the 2 that spawn initially with the boss. These adds hit around 1,000 and can also be rooted. You can either kill the adds first (undergeared players should kill adds first), or CC them and continue to kill the boss.

The first 2 mobs never seem to be mezzable, but sometimes can be rootable; the final 4 mobs seem to be random whether they are rootable or mezzable, meaning it can change each time you do the trial.

Phase 2 — The Three Branches

After completing the first phase each area allows you to portal into the phase 2 area. It is split into 3 branches, Earth and Air being together, Water and Fire being together, and Undead being on its own. Each branch starts with a good chunk of trash appropriate to the branch (Earth branch will have Rock mobs and so on). When the last of the trash is killed a second wave of trash spawns along with a Boss at the end. You are given 1 hour (plus any leftover) to complete all 5 branches. Lockouts from this point forward are 5.5 days.

Phase 3 — The Trash Clear

After completing the second phase all the branches reunite. This phase is a lengthy clearing fest of 8 waves, each with a bunch of trash (pure melee) and 2 bosses. When the bosses of wave 8 die, two massive rock golems spawn at the portal. You are granted a total of 75 minutes (plus any leftover) to complete this phase.

Quarm Script

Sourced from the Quarm server Lua script (potimeb/encounters/Phase3.lua): Eight waves spawn in sequence, each with two bosses plus a group of adds. The two wave-8 bosses are the massive rock golems (Supernatural Guardian and Avatar of the Elements). Shadow names are defined for all 18 bosses; killing the wave-8 bosses at the portal completes the phase.

After completing the third phase you move on to the first big challenges. To start you need to clear out the trash from the central area and then take on Saryrn, Terris-Thule, Vallon Zek, and Tallon Zek. You are granted 4 hours (plus any leftover) to defeat them and can do so in any order.
