The Planes of Power

Flagging

The complete planar progression checklist — NPC dialogue, boss kills, and zoning steps for every plane, plus how to fix flags with Seer Mal Nae`Shi and Grand Librarian Maelin.

Bosses — Tier 1

Tier 1 raids: the Justice Trials in Plane of Justice, Grummus in Plane of Disease, Terris-Thule in Plane of Nightmare, and the Manaetic Behemoth with Xanamech Nezmirthafen in Plane of Innovation.

Bosses — Tier 2

Tier 2 raids: Aerin`Dar in Plane of Valor; Bertoxxulous and Carprin Deatharn (Event) in Crypt of Decay; Saryrn and The Keeper of Sorrows (Event) in Plane of Torment.

Bosses — Tier 3

Tier 3 raids: Agnarr the Storm Lord in Bastion of Thunder, the Halls of Honor trials, Lord Mithaniel Marr in Temple of Marr, and Rallos Zek with Tallon Zek and Vallon Zek in Plane of Tactics.

Tower of Solusek Ro

The tower's named encounters — Xuzl, Arlyxir, Jiva, Rizlona, and The Protector of Desolik — leading to Solusek Ro himself.

Plane of Air

The avatars and rings of Plane of Air, plus the Xegony raid.

Plane of Earth

Traps and group nameds in Plane of Earth A, its ring events, the mini bosses of Plane of Earth B, and the Rathe Council.

Plane of Fire

The mini bosses of Plane of Fire and Fennin Ro.

Plane of Water

The mini bosses of Plane of Water and Coirnav.

Plane of Time

The final raid, from Phase 1 through Phase 5 + Quarm.

Videos

Video guides and footage covering flagging, bosses, raids, and quests across the expansion.
