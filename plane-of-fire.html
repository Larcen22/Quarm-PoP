Plane of Fire

The plane bosses, the Fennin Ro army event, and Fennin Ro the Tyrant of Fire

Overview

All Plane of Fire bosses are Immune to Fire

The ten plane bosses are covered in their own sections below; the Fennin Ro army event and the Tyrant of Fire himself are covered in the Ring Events and Fennin Ro sections.

Ring Events

The Fennin Ro Event

Below is a map of the general layout of the event.

Guardian of Doomfire

Expansion: Planes of Power · Zone: Plane of Fire · Level: 70 · Phase 1

Kill Guardian of Doomfire to start the event. He has approx 3 day respawn.

Fight Info

  • 150,000 HP (PQDI; eqprogression estimated ~175K)
  • Hits 238–1515 (PQDI; eqprogression estimated 1500+)
  • Slowable
  • Flurries

Quarm Script

Sourced from the Quarm server Lua script (pofire/encounters/Fennin.lua, SecretsOTheP/quests):

  • Phase 1 of the army event — defeating the Guardian of Doomfire (217050) triggers the first army wave: 17 Rage Reavers of Flame (217417), 6 Chaos Healers of Flame (217418), 8 Dark Magi of Flame (217419), and 3 each of Doomfire Darkfiend (217421), Doomfire Chaosfiend (217420), and Doomfire Ragefiend (217422), spawned at fixed locations along the field.
  • The Guardian's death also starts the event failure timer (~4 hours 52 minutes), which pauses while any event mob is in combat and resumes once combat ends.
  • On timer expiry (failure), all event mobs are depop'd and the Guardian's spawn (367088) is set to respawn in 18 hours.
  • On event success (Fennin Ro defeated), the same spawn is instead set to respawn in 5 days 18 hours.

Trash Packs

Phase 2 · 9 packs

Kill the trash packs (smaller yellow circles on the above map). They have a small aggro radius so in some cases they won’t all aggro at once. The doomfire “demon models” are rootable. They rampage and can hit for 2000+. The packs are all slowable. There are 9 packs (if I counted right).

Quarm Script

Sourced from the Quarm server Lua script (pofire/encounters/SnareImmunity.lua, SecretsOTheP/quests):

  • Covers 8 field trash types: obsidian tree spiders (217048, 217455), obsidian tree spiderling (217002), fiery spirit steed (217423), jopal hunter (217007), jopal gatherer (217028), flame lord (217029), flame lordling (217006), and jopal lavahurler (217047).
  • On spawn, each mob has a 20% chance to gain special ability 16 (snare) set to 1, making it immune to snares.
  • A single shared spawn handler applies the check to all eight types rather than duplicating the mechanic per mob.

The Four Lords

Phase 3 · Level 78

Once you clear all the trash packs 4x named and some additional trash will spawn. Keep the raid away from them so the pullers have room to split.

Council of Fire

Phase 4 · Level 78

At this point your raid can move up to the entrance of Fennin Ro’s castle. This phase consists of killing another 4x named mobs (pretty trivial) that can be pulled out of Fennin’s castle.

Once the 4th mob dies two things happen:

  1. Fennin Ro spawns on his throne
  2. Some demons will spawn blocking the bridge preventing further players from running in. You can still Call of Hero or corpse drag.

Chancellor Kirtra

  • 230,000 HP (PQDI; eqprogression estimated 250K)
  • Hits 551–1450 (PQDI; eqprogression estimated 1500+)
  • Slowable
  • Casts Web of Flame (PBAE, Root/-50 Mana Drain DoT, -150 Fire Based) 1 min CD

Quarm Script

Sourced from the Quarm server Lua script (pofire/encounters/Fennin.lua, SecretsOTheP/quests):

  • Phase 3 of the army event — Chancellor Kirtra (217432) spawns at a fixed location near Fennin's castle once all phase 2 mobs (the Four Lords and their wave) are dead.
  • Spawned alongside the other three council guardians: Chancellor Traxom (217433), Omni Magus Crato (217429), and Warlord Prollaz (217428).
  • When all four council guardians are dead, Fennin Ro (217440) spawns on his throne and ten elite guardian spawns (369529–369538) are enabled to hold the bridge.

Fennin Ro the Tyrant of Fire

Fennin Ro the Tyrant of Fire

Expansion: Planes of Power · Zone: Plane of Fire · Level: 80

Phase 5 — The Final Fight

Time to kill Fennin Ro! Most guilds tank him in the outside corner of the steps. This is due to his AE rampage. New PoP guilds might want to consider a Complete Heal chain. Overgeared guilds can spam heals on the tank. Melee should stay max range. He has an AE DD and Mana Drain as well (see below).

Fight Info

  • 1,000,000 HP (PQDI; eqprogression estimated 1.1 Million)
  • Hits 1048–1962 (PQDI; eqprogression estimated 2500+)
  • Slowable
  • AE Rampage
  • Casts Cataclysm of Ro (PBAE, 3300 DD/-100 Mana Drain DoT/100 HP DoT, -1000 Fire Based, 18 curse cure)

Quarm Script

Sourced from the Quarm server Lua script (pofire/encounters/Fennin.lua, SecretsOTheP/quests):

  • The 4-phase army event concludes here: Guardian of Doomfire → army waves → Four Lords → Council of Fire → Fennin Ro. Fennin (217440) spawns on his throne when all four council guardians are dead, and ten elite guardian spawns (369529–369538) are enabled to hold the bridge.
  • The failure timer started by the Guardian's death (~4 hours 52 minutes, paused during combat) gains a one-time +2 hour extension (once per encounter) when Fennin spawns.
  • On timer expiry (failure), all event mobs are depop'd, the elites are disabled, and the Guardian's spawn (367088) is set to respawn in 18 hours.
  • On success, the Guardian's spawn is instead set to respawn in 5 days 18 hours.
  • Fennin's death ends the event and spawns the Essence of Fire (217454) at his position, flagged to the killer with kill rights (say “hail” for Globe of Dancing Flame or Quintessence of Elements, up to 72 flags, despawns 20 min out of combat).
