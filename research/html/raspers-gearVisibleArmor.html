Visible Armor

Bor Wharhammer in the Plane of Tranquility is the dwarf to see about quested armor. If you are at least level 55, for 500 platinum he will give you a container and an emblem specific to your class. Inside the container place your Mold/Pattern, 1-3 of the appropriate ethereal pieces, and a tanaan smithy hammer or tanaan embroidery needle. Plate classes will use ethereal sheet of metal, chain classes will need ethereal metal rings, leather uses cured ethereal energy, and silk uses ethereal silk swatches. The number of pieces is based on the slot, 1 for wrists, 2 for hands, head, arms, and feet, and 3 for legs and chest. Leather however is just 1 for all except 2 for legs and chest. The materials require 220 tailoring or smithing to create, but are tradable and the armor combine is no fail.

Ornate molds are tradable and found in Tier 3 zones. Elemental molds are no-trade and found on Tier 4 raids.
