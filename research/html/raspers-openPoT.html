Plane of Torment

Overview

The Plane of Torment is a Tier 2 zone, requires defeating Terris Thule for access. This is the home of Saryrn, but serves little purpose beyond raiding: the mobs are poor xp, can make pulling difficult with snare, and the Rare mobs have no loot. Even the tradeskilled gear made from zone drops aren't worth the effort.

Events

Keying for Saryrn
  • Size: 2-3+ groups suggested
  • Respawn: N/A

To start this you need to agro Baraguj Szuul on the upper floor of his building and allow him to hit you, this will teleport your group into his stomach. When everybody is inside open the trapdoor in the floor and slide to face off with some zone trash until you reach An Unimaginable Horror, flurries about 500 and has 100k hp. Once it is dead you will be ported out. The port out requires you to be hit by an invisible mob, so make sure you don't have runes up. Outside the raid will be downstairs to face Baraguj, for real this time. Baraguj drops Mouths of Baraguj Szuul, a 4 slot container, and one of the following:

  • Slippers of Eternal Nightmares
  • Rapier of Curses
  • Shadowhide Gloves
  • Band of Miserable Souls

Once you have the container you will need to hunt down 4 rare spawns: The Avatar of Agony, Anguish, Pain, and Suffering. Each Avatar drops their respective Orb. Combine the four orbs inside the Mouths to form a Screaming Sphere which is the key to access Saryn's Tower.

Salczek the Fleshgrinder
  • Size: 54
  • Respawn: 3 days

Spawns on 1st floor of the Tower. Late in the fight he gains AE rampage. Drops a spell or two and sometimes one of the following:

  • Dirk of Great Sorrow
  • Aegis of Gloom
  • Tormented Flute
  • Baton of Torment
The Acolyte of Affliction
  • Size: 54
  • Respawn: 3 days

Spawns on 2nd floor of the Tower. Spawns adds during the fight which despawn when he dies. Drops a spell or two and sometimes one of the following:

  • Adorned Soulstone Mask
  • Black Cloak of Malise
  • Whip of Unending Pain
  • Staff of Agonizing Battle
Ta'Grusch the Abomination
  • Size: 54
  • Respawn: 3 days

Spawns on 3rd floor of the Tower. Very low health but deals serious rampage damage. Drops a spell or two and sometimes one of the following:

  • Staff of Primordial Ooze
  • Tormented Stone of Souls
  • Girdle of the Tormentor
  • Shield of Unimaginable Horrors
Maareq the Prophet
  • Size: 54
  • Respawn: 3 days

Spawns in the Tower. Once engaged a stream of "minion of Maareq" move towards him and heal if they reach. They can not be stunned or mezzed, so bring plenty of AE nukers. Late in the fight he transforms into one of the 4 tentacle mouth things and gains AE rampage. Drops a spell or two and sometimes one of the following:

  • Lesser Eye of Insanity
  • Battlecloak of Lost Souls
  • Longblade of Ethereal Terror
  • Shadow-Woven Sleeves
The Keeper of Sorrows
  • Size: 54
  • Respawn: 3 days

After hailing Tylis (keyword: we are ready) the raid will be ported over (make sure everybody is close so to not get left behind). Once inside you'll have a few rooms of trash to clear before reaching Keeper. It normally drops a spell and sometimes one of the following:

  • Band of the Adorned
  • Great Blade of Malise
  • Crowned Staff of Anguish
  • Tormentor's Chain Collar
  • Shaded Cloak
Saryrn
  • Size: 54
  • Respawn: 3 days

Spawns at the top of the Tower with her pet raven Sorrowsong. During the fight Sorrowsong moves around casting a PBAE silence that you'll need to avoid. Meanwhile every 10% Saryn will spawn 3 adds that need to be rooted away from the raid. Once Saryn is down you can clean up adds and hail the planar projection for your flag. Drops a couple spells antd two of the following:

  • Orb of Infinite Terror
  • Unadorned Cloak of Battle
  • Twin-Blade of Sorrow
  • Miserable Bauble
  • Greatstaff of Misery
  • Cape Embroidered with Runes
