Plane of Fire

Overview

The Plane of Fire is a Tier 4 zone which requires all flags to access. Mobs here are, unsurprisingly, immune to fire. The trash hsa a mixture of see invis so be careful when moving. A couple raid mobs are sprinkled around the zone, be sure to give them a wide berth when grouping.

Events

General Druav Flamesinger
  • Size: 3+ groups
  • Respawn: 1 day

This guy hits for up to 2100, and procs a 3k dd with stun. Drops one of the following.:

  • Lava Forged Face Guard
  • Burning Mask of Terror
  • Ornate Greatblade

Criare Sunmane and Jaxoliz Dawneyes
  • Size: 4+ groups
  • Respawn: 1 day

The phoenixes, commonly called the charm birds. Hit for around 2k and casts a charm. Both drop two of the following:

  • Shimmering Sleeves of Flame
  • Shining Crimson Shawl
  • Drape of the Agile
  • Fang of Flame
  • Staff of Eternal Flames

Arch Mage Yozanni
  • Size: full raid
  • Respawn: 3 days

This guy's main difficulty comes from a PBAE that is an attack and AC debuff along with a 250 per tick mana drain and 18 curse counters. He casts this every 30 seconds, so be sure to have Greater Remove Curse ready to keep your healers going. Beyond that just dps through his health. Drops two of the following:

  • Elemental Helm Molds
  • Elemental Glove Molds
  • Phoenix Claw Talisman

Blazzax the Omnifiend
  • Size: full raid
  • Respawn: 3 days

A big demon. AE is a straightforward 2k dd. Drops two of the following:

  • Elemental Wrist Molds
  • Elemental Boot Molds
  • Cape of Flames

General Reparm
  • Size: full raid
  • Respawn: 3 days

This one hits for nearly 3k and procs a 3k dd with stun. Drops two of the following:

  • Elemental Helm Molds
  • Elemental Boot Molds
  • Fiery Staff of Zha

Magmaton
  • Size: full raid
  • Respawn: 3 days

Pure melee golem hits for about 2700, rampages, and can't be slowed. Drops two of the following:

  • Elemental Wrist Molds
  • Elemental Boot Molds
  • Necklace of Reflected Flames

Quavonis Firetail
  • Size: full raid
  • Respawn: 3 days

Another phoenix. This one hits for nearly 3k and casts a 500 dd that shadowsteps moving the entire raid around. Drops two of the following:

  • Elemental Sleeve Molds
  • Cloak of Combustion

Babnoxis the Spider Queen
  • Size: full raid

The big spider boss. Has an AE 500 dot and 125 per tick mana drain. 18 poison counters. Drops two of the following:

  • Elemental Wrist Molds
  • Elemental Boot Molds
  • Elemental Glove Molds
  • Mask of the Insightful

Pyronis
  • Size: full raid

Raid frog hits for about 2500 and casts a 1500 dd with stun every minute. Drops two of the following:

  • Elemental Wrist Molds
  • Elemental Helm Molds
  • Elemental Boot Molds
  • Blackflame Sphere

Fennin Ro the Tyrant of Fire
  • Size: 54
  • Respawn: 3 days

The boss of Fire. To start the script kill the Guardian of Doomfire (flurries). Start pulling trash headed toward Fennin, be wary that the "doomfire chaosfiend" will rampage. Next up are 4 named demon mobs, take them one at a time. Azobian the Darklord (casts an AE blind), Hebabbilys the Ragelord (hits 3k and comes buffed with haste/attack, be sure to dispel), Javonn the Overlord (hits 2k and casts a 100 range AE spinstun), and Reaxnous the Chaoslord (hits 1500, flurries, casts a 1k rain). These four each have a chance of dropping one of the following:

  • Band of Elder Understanding
  • Sable Shoes of Flame
  • Fire Marked Rune
  • Blaring Horn of Fire

With those down you can move up a bit for the next two mini-bosses, Chancellor Traxom (phoenix, hits 1500, casts a blind) and Chancellor Kirta (spider, hit 1300, casts an AE root with small mana drain). These can also drop one of the following:

  • Bracer of Flickering Flames
  • Fire Crested Rune
  • Cord of the Pyre
  • Flame Idol of Ro

Move up a bit more, clear a few more out includin Warlord Prollaz (nothing special) and Omni Magus Crato (Casts a 3k dd and 500 mana drain AE, can drop same loot as Chancellors) and you're ready to pull Fennin himself. Fennin hits around 1900, AE rampages, and casts an AE that does 3k, 100 dot, and 100 mana drain per tick (18 curse counters) every minute. When killed you wil get an Globe of Dancing Flame, one of the 4 pieces needed for Plane of Time key. Has a chance of dropping an Elemental Leg pattern as a bonus along with 2 of the following:

  • Gleaming Mesh Girdle
  • Great Mask of Flame
  • Immaculate Vest of the Fire Tyrant
  • Earring of Eternal Flame
  • Ifir, Dagger of Fire
