Plane of Disease

Overview

The Plane of Disease is a Tier 1 zone. Grimror Plaguebringer roams around near the zone in, offering the alternate access quest to Crypt of Decay. To the south is a cave system with spiders and bubonians, to the north is a field with larvae (which drop the bits for alternate CoD access), and to the west is Grummus' castle.

  • Zi/Zo - Zone in, Zone out
  • 1 - a diseased infested bubonian
  • 2 - a malarian metamo
  • 3 - an arachnae parian
  • 4 - a sengian fly (roams)
  • A - Aramin the Spider Guardian
  • G - Grummus

Events

Rallius Rattican
  • Size: 2+ groups suggested
  • Respawn: 3 days

Rallius can be found in the back of the cave to the south. He has a good number of guards in his room and when agro'd he activates spider traps heading out of the caves. If you can have a bard DA pull him out of the caves and get him solo, it is a pushover. If you are going to engage on the spot plan on dealing with his pet spider. He always drops Rattican's Head for the PoK Manual quests, a spell scroll, and one of the following:
  • Decomposing Wristband
  • Arachnid Antennae
  • Gloves of Flowing Ooze
  • Pox Ridden Carapace
  • Cursed Armplates
  • Oozing Quarterstaff
Gryme the Crypt Guardian
  • Size: 2+ groups suggested
  • Respawn: Unknown

Gryme is found at the entrance to the castle. You'll need to kill him on the way to Grummus as his only drop is Gryme's Crypt Key which opens the locked door to Grummus. Be aware the key is No Rent.
Aramin the Spider Guardian
  • Size: 2+ groups suggested
  • Respawn: 1 day?

Aramin comes after Gryme as you move through the castle. It is permarooted so clear the room first. When killed a bunch of small spiders will spawn so be sure nobody is sitting. Aramin drops a spell scroll and two of the following:
  • Gooey Boots
  • Signet of Withering Flesh
  • Bracer of Rotting Bile
  • Disease Imbued Greatstaff
  • Firebone Notebook
  • Orb of Unidentifiable Pulp
Grummus
  • Size: 54
  • Respawn: 3 days

Grummus is the boss of Disease. He has two AEs that need to be cured (curse and disease) and a single target slow (keep tank cured). When killed he drops four spells and two of the following:
  • Bone Spiked Crusher
  • Chunk of Decaying Cloth
  • Diseased Ratskin Mask
  • Fetid Flesh Wristband
  • Malarian Leg Ear Stud
  • Pus Covered Beetle Carapace
  • Ring of the Pox-Bearer
  • Runed Lance of Gryme
