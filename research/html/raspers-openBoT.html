Rare Mobs

The rares in BoT share loot tables across their wing, so all Earth rares have the same drops, except for a specific ornate armor piece. It is "possible" that rares can drop other types of patterns, but if so it is so rare as to have no impact. All PH for rare mobs are "a xyz kriger".

Events

Keying for Towers
  • Size: 1 group
  • Respawn: N/A

Collect a Ring of Torden, a four slot container, from any of the four wing bosses. Then get a Gem from trash mobs in each wing: Sandstorm Gem (south), Blizzard Gem (east), Tornado Gem (north), and Lightning Gem (west). Combine them all to get an Enchanted Ring of Torden. This gives you the ability to port a group into the four towers from the Courtyard.

Keying for Agnarr
  • Size: raid
  • Respawn: N/A

Collect a Unadorned Symbol of Torden, a ten slot container, from any of the tower raid bosses. Then get a spheres from trash in each Tower, combine them to get a Symbol of Torden, which will let a raid in to fight Agnarr from the Courtyard.

Kuanbyr Hailstorm
  • Size: 3+ groups
  • Respawn: 6 hours?

Boss of the Sandstone Tower (northeast). Spawns with 3 stormriders Hrani, Leistr, and Iri. Leister has lower health but can not be slowed, Hrani can be rooted, and Iri has a lot of health but can be mezzed. Hailstorm himself has some short range AE stuns. Drops Unadorned Symbol of Torden for the Agnarr key quest, a spell or two, and two of the following:

  • Ornate Silk Robe Pattern
  • Stormriders Signet
  • Greater Stormwatcher Horn
  • Gargoyle Talon

Agnarr the Stormlord
  • Size: 54
  • Respawn: 3 days

Boss of Bastion of Thunder. Don't have much info on this raid. You clear floors with mobs spawning from portals while wizards disentegrate the portals directly. Then you engage Agnarr. He has two AEs, one that is a long stun the other a dd + mana drain. Additionally you get a giant add every 25%. When Agnarr goes down you can go speak with Karana to get your flag, it is very easy to miss this step and then you'll need to kill Agnarr again! Drops a few spells and two of the following:

  • Double Edged Stormblade
  • Crystallized Rain
  • Stormriders Gauntlets
  • Karana's Tear
  • Amulet of the Rain
  • Longbow of the Stormcaller
  • Orb of Thunderous Spirits
