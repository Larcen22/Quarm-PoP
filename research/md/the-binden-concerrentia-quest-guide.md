The Binden Concerrentia Quest Guide
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/The_Binden_Concerrentia/The-Binden-Concerrentia.jpg | alt=
The Bindin Concerrentia allows you to gate to the “good” side of Plane of Knowledge. It uses Quintessence of Knowledge as a reagent but has unlimited uses. These can be made by Shaman’s Alchemy for approx. 105pp each. Trivial is only 15 skill. This is a great item for melee, especially for those with bad origin locations.
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/The_Binden_Concerrentia/The-Binden-Concerrentia-Logo.jpg | alt=
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/The_Binden_Concerrentia/Vial.jpg | alt=
