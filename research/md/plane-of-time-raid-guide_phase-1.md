You can have 18 people per trial. All 5 need to be completed within 1 hour to advanced to Phase 2 Plane of Time.

Air Trial
Air Trial is a quick trial but requires some CC and/or aggro management. All mobs in this trial can be slowed.
There is a total of 2 Waves:
Wave 1
4x An Air Phoenix Noble spawn. They can be either rootable, mezzable, or neither. It can be different each time.
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/PoAirWave1.jpg | alt=
Wave 2
4x Servitor of Xegony spawn along with the boss, Neimon of Air. All of these will spawn along the back wall where the door is. The 2 inner ones can be mezzed, the outer 2 can be rooted or off-tanked.
You can kill the mobs in Wave 2 whatever order is easiest for your raid. For us it is usually easiest to kill the Neimon of Air first, then kill the rooted/off-tanked birds, then the mezzed birds. You’ll want to dispel Neimon of Air as well (see his stat info below).
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/PoAirWave2.jpg | alt=
Boss Info:
Neimon of Air– 240K HP– Caustic Aura (Self Buff, +100 All Resists, + 125 ATK, +125 Damage Shield) — dispel it– Caustic Atmosphere (PBAE, -25% Spell Haste/Melee Haste, 550, 3 min CD, -150 Poison Resist)– Hits 1300+ (35 Heroic Stamina)– Slowable– AE Rampage
Earth Trial
Everything in the Earth Trial is slowable. It can take a while so bring a decent amount of DPS to this trial to get it done in a timely manner.
This trial starts off with 3x a pile of living rubble spawning. They cannot be mezzed or rooted so need to be tanked. Each time you kill one, some 2x to 4x a rock shaped assassin will spawn. They can be mezzed but not rooted. Kill them off then move onto the next a pile of living rubble.
After the 6th a rock shaped assassin dies, Terlok of Earth spawns. Kill him to win the trial then finish off any remaining trash mobs.
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/Earth-Trial.jpg | alt=
Boss Info:
Terlok of Earth– 290K HP– Hits 1300+ (35 Heroic Stamina)– Slowable– Rampages
Fire Trial
Everything in this trial can be slowed. Fire trial has a few different waves.
Wave 1
It starts off with 3x a flame mephit (big mephits) spawning. Each time you kill one 2x inferno mephit spawn (small mephits). Kill them before moving onto the next big mephit. The big mephits cannot be mezzed, and can only sometimes be rooted. The small mephits can sometimes be mezzed or rooted.
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/Fire-Wave-1.jpg | alt=
Wave 2
Once all trash is dead the boss, Kazrok of Fire will spawn. You’ll want to dispel his buff and get him slowed ASAP. He will spawn 2 additional adds at 75%, 50%, and 25%. a flame mephit is mezzable. an inferno mephit is rootable.
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/Fire-Wave-2.jpg | alt=
Boss Info:
Kazrok of Fire– 230K HP– Pyrokinetic Aura (Self Buff, +150 All Resists/+75 Damage Shield/+175 ATK, dispelable)– Black Pyre (PBAE, -50% Spell Haste, 225 DoT, -150 Fire Based, 3 min recast)– Hits 1300+ (35 Heroic Stamina)– Slowable– Rampages
Undead Trial
The Undead Trial has 4 Waves. Everything is slowable in this trial.
Wave 1
3x an undead guardian spawn (Level 64). They cannot be mezzed but can be rooted.
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/UndeadWave1.jpg | alt=
Wave 2
4x an undead guardian spawn (Level 65). They cannot be mezzed but can be rooted.
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/UndeadWave2.jpg | alt=
Wave 3
4x an undead guardian spawn (Yellow to 65). They can be mezzed.

Wave 4
2x an undead protector and the boss, Rythor of the Undead spawn.

Water Trial
Water Trial is essentially one long wave. The boss, Anar of Water spawns right away, along with 2x a triloun of water adds. Anar of Water cannot be slowed, but the adds can.
The boss will also spawn another 2x a deepwater triloun mobs at roughly 90% and 45% HP. This means a total of 6 adds if you count the 2 that spawn initially with the boss. You can either kill the adds first (undergeared players should kill adds first), or CC them and continue to kill the boss.
The first 2 mobs never seem to be mezzable, but sometimes can be rootable, the final 4 mobs seem to be random whether they are rootable or mezzable, meaning it can change each time you do the trial.
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/Water-Trial.jpg | alt=
Boss Info:
Anar of Water– 290K HP– Aqueous Flux (PBAE, 850 DD/70% Snare/-40% Attack Speed, 5 min recast, Cold Based)– Hits 1300+ (35 Heroic Stamina)– Cannot be slowed– Rampages
