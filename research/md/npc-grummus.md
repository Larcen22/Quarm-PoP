Grummus
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/Grummus_Guide/Grummus1.png | alt=
Expansion: Planes of PowerZone: Plane of Disease MAPLevel: 70Respawn Timer: Estimated 2-3 days. Can also be spawned via Agent of Change.Notes: Grummus is required to progress in PoP Flagging.
Fight Info
Grummus is a raid boss located in the back of the castle. There are two mini-bosses before him. He considered an easy fight and is a pushover for Level 60+ mid sized raids. Group heals and disease cures are helpful to counteract his DoT’s.
– Hits 1500+– Slowable– Rampages– Approx. 500K HP with MoTM– Casts Decay of the Plaguebringer (Single Target, 300 HP/Tick DoT + 60% Slow, Disease Based)– Casts Stench of Decay (PBAE, 200 HP DoT, Disease Based)– Casts Plasma Decay (PBAE, DoT with damage that increases more each tick, Disease Based)
If PoP Flagging: Once Grummus is dead, hail the Planar Projection for a character flag. Click on the sewer cap to reveal the the zone in to Crypt of Decay. Drop down.
