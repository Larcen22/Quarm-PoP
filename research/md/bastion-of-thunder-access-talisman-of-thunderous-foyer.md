Talisman of Thunderous Foyer GuideBastion of Thunder Access
This is not an actual key but a PoP flag that shows up as an achievement. It is a required part of Planar Progression. The quest takes place in Plane of Storms. When PoP first releases you’ll want a group for most of this, and 1-2 groups for the named.
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoP_Planar_Flagging/BoTKeyFlag.png | alt=
Step 1 – Farming
You’ll first need to farm the following items:
1x Storm Giant Head 1x Storm Volaas Beard 1x Storm Satuur Sash 1x Storm Taarid Bone 2x Esoteric Medallion

Esoteric Medallion (Collect 2)

These drop off named giants in each of the camps. You’ll need 2. They are Lore, but ID differently (North, South, and West). This means you must get 1 from at least 2 of the 3 camps. The mini bosses are 1-2 groupable, with the exception of the Lord’s, which are raid bosses, so it is not recommended to trying to 1 group those.

Step 2 – Dialogue
1) Locate Askr the Lost (-1250, -2600) and give him Storm Giant Head. 2) Say “continue” twice to receive Askr’s Bag of Verity (container).3) Combine Storm Volaas Beard, Storm Satuur Sash, and Storm Taarid Bone in Askr’s Bag of Verity (container) to create Askr’s Sealed Bag of Verity.4) Give Askr’s Sealed Bag of Verity to Askr to Lost (receive character flag)5) Say “bastion of thunder” to receive another Askr’s Bag of Verity (container).6) Combine 2x Esoteric Medallion in Askr’s Bag of Verity (container) to receive Esotoric Meld.7) Give Esotoric Meld to to Askr to Lost (receive character flag)8) Run to the middle of the zone and click on the portal to Bastion of Thunder (receive character flag)
