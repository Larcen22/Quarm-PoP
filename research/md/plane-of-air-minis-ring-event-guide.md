Plane of Air Mini Boss/Ring Event Guide
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/Wind_Etched_Key_Xegony/Xegony-Key-Map2.jpg | alt=
There are a total of 4 ring events in Plane of Air. At the end of each event an “Avatar” spawns.
Use the map above to correlate the ring events to their respective Avatar. They are in the following order:
Stormrider Ring Event – Spawns Avatar of Wind Elemental Ring Event – Spawns Avatar of Smoke Phoenix Ring Event – Spawns Avatar of Mist Spider Ring Event – Spawns Avatar of Dust
Before you reach any event you’ll come across the small boss Baltaldor the Cursed. Be careful when pulling him because he’s packed in tight with some trash mobs. He’s trivial with a full raid force.
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/Xegony_Guide/Baltaldor-the-Cursed.jpg | alt=
– Hits 1800+ – 300K HP – Slowable – AE Rampages – Casts self Memory Blur (drops aggro) – Casts Diseased Cloud (Targeted AE, 55% Slow/1250 DD, -150 Disease Based, 9 disease counter) – Casts Hail Storm (PBAE, 400 DD + 1 second stun. Magic Based)
Stormrider Ring Event – Spawns Avatar of Wind
VIDEO GUIDE
1) Clear all the mobs on the island. 2) Once the island is clear a script starts and some stormriders spawn in a formation in front of Stormrider Priest of Destruction. The stormriders will become attackable in waves of 2. Once all are dead you can then kill Stormrider Priest of Destruction. It is pretty easy and has about 150K HP.
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/Xegony_Guide/stormriders.jpg | alt=
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/Xegony_Guide/stormrider-priest-of-desctruction.jpg | alt=
3) Some time will go by (5-10 minutes). You may see a mob spawn every now and then during this time. Just kill them as they pop.
4) Eventually the final wave of mobs will spawn along with the rings final boss: Pherlondien Clawpike. Kill all the trash (also called Pherlondien Clawpike, but are lower level) and higher level Pherlondien Clawpike to spawn Avatar of Wind.
You’ll see the emote: Avatar of Wind shouts, ‘I’ll make sure that no mortal is allowed to defile this sacred realm!’
Pherlondien Clawpike fight Info:
– 500K HP – Hits 2K+ – Slowable – AE Rampage

[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/Xegony_Guide/pherlondien-clawpike.jpg | alt=
Elemental Ring Event – Spawns Avatar of Smoke
VIDEO GUIDE
1) Clear all the mobs on the island.
2) When the last mob dies 4x an elemental champion spawn. Kill them. They hit for around 1,000. Somewhere around 100K-200K HP each. Slowable. My parse on the champions seems off for HP.
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/Xegony_Guide/a-elemental-champion.jpg | alt=
3) When the last an elemental champion dies, The Elemental Masterpiece spawns. Kill it to spawn Avatar of Smoke.
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/Xegony_Guide/The-Elemental-masterpiece.jpg | alt=
You’ll see the emote: Avatar of Smoke shouts, ‘The time has come for all intruders to be tested! I will not allow you to pillage this plane any longer!’
The Elemental Masterpiece fight info:
– 600K HP – Hits 2100+ – Slowable – Rampages
Phoenix Ring Event – Spawns Avatar of Mist
VIDEO GUIDE
1) Clear all the mobs on the island. Calebgrothiel may also spawn. Easy named.
2) Once all of the mobs are dead 4x a phoenix firesurger spawn in the middle. You can kill them one at time.
3) Once all the firesurgers are dead 4x a phoenix windsurger will spawn. You can kill them one at time.
4) Once the final windsurger dies Melernil Faal`Armanna will spawn. Kill it to spawn Avatar of Mist.
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/Xegony_Guide/melernil.jpg | alt=
Melernil Faal`Armanna fight info:
– 600K HP – Hits 2300+ – Slowable – Rampage – Flurry – Casts Wave of White Noise (PBAE, Silence, Unresistable, 45 sec recast)
You’ll see the emote: Avatar of Mist shouts, ‘Leave now, foolish mortals! You have set in motion events that you cannot possibly understand!’
Spider Ring Event – Spawns Avatar of Dust
VIDEO GUIDE
It is recommended to fight in the tunnel rather than on the island for this event. Spiders hit pretty hard and can stun so it’s important not to have too many at once.
1) Clear all the mobs on the island.
2) When the last spider dies the event starts. Small level 50 spiders (an erratic arachnid) will continuously spawn until the end of the event. There is a max number of them – roughly 15. Enchanters will want to keep these AE mezzed until the end of the event. There is no point in killing them as they will just respawn.
3) In the meantime the rest of the raid should be killing the a vorladien archwalker. They have approx. 180K HP each and can spin stun. archwalkers hit for 1300+. Everything is slowable.
– Kill one to spawn 3x a vorladien spawn. These hit 1400+ and HP range from 50K to 150K each. – Kill 3x a vorladien spawn spawn the next a vorladien archwalker. – Repeat this 3 times. After the 3rd round Sigismond Windwalker spawns under the rainbow. Kill it to spawn Avatar of Dust.
All the small adds that are being mezzed will then despawn.
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/Xegony_Guide/supa-spida.jpg | alt=
Sigismond Windwalker fight info:
– 650K HP – Hits 2200+ – Slowable – AE Rampage – Flurries (rare) – Casts Wave of White Noise (PBAE, Silence, Unresistable, 45 sec recast) – Casts Air Strike (Single, 3850 DD, Unresistable, 30 sec recast)
You’ll see the emote: Avatar of Dust shouts, ‘Elements of Air and Dust, I call upon you to aid me in the defense of this land!’
