Step 1
Farm 4x Items in Planes of Power Zones:
Bundle of Super Conductive Wires (NO TRADE): trash mobs in PoInnovation. “models” are a good mob to farm. Uncommon Drop. Silicorrosive Grease – trash mobs in PoInnovation. Uncommon Drop. Gold-Tipped Boar Horn – “boar” type mobs in PoTactics. Uncommon to Semi-rare Drop? Shard of Pure Energy – Fire Tower mobs in BoThunder, may also drop from other mobs but not confirmed. Uncommon Drop.
