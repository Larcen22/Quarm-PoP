Tallon Zek
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/Tallon-Zek-cut.png | alt=
You’ll want to kill Tallon Zek before killing Vallon Zek so you have the room required for the Vallon Zek boss fight later. Tallon Zek is a tank and spank as in he will put out huge amounts of damage on the main tank. Defensive Discipline highly recommended here. A Complete Heal chain works best for this boss. He can also put out some nasty AE damage, especially if facing the raid. It is recommended that the main tank also stack up some junk debuffs at the top of their buff list. Be careful not to aggro Vallon Zek when fightning Tallon Zek, this is a common mistake in raids that can lead to a wipe.
He casts Planeshift (see spell info below), this has a 7 minute cooldown so you can pull/leash him if you want (with the raid far away) to trigger it before engaging.
– 1.5 Million HP – Hits 3500+ (35 Heroic Stamina)– Cannot be slowed– Rampages– Casts Planeshift (PBAE, 50% Increased Mana Cost of spells, -200 Magic Based, 7 min recast)– Casts 1 of 4 different types of Barb of Tallon every 5 seconds: Targeted AE, -300 HP DoT/-150 AC/60% Deaggro on Main Tank, -100 Disease Based)Targeted AE, 1500-3500 DD, -100 Cold Based, 40% Slow, 40% SnareSingle Target, 3500 DD, -100 Fire Based, 5 second recast)Single Target, 1750 DD, -100 Poison Based) – Casts Tallon’s Balance (PBAE, -300 All Resists, Unresistable, 60 sec recast) – Casts Strategic Blow (Single Target, 2000 DD/500 DoT/Stun, -200 Cold Based, 6 min recast)
Vallon Zek
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/Vallon-Zek-cut.png | alt=
You’ll want to kill Tallon Zek before doing Vallon Zek so you have room to deal with the adds from this boss.
Vallon Zek will Feign Death and Stun the main tank every 24 seconds so you’ll want to have multiple tanks on him at once all generating aggro. Once the Tank gets Feign Death’d another tank will need to to pick him up. For this reason a Complete Heal chain isn’t effective on this guy, so the raid will need to spam heal the tanks. He also casts a PBAE debuff on the raid that will increase the aggro of raid members so DPS will need to watch their aggro.
At 45-50% HP range he will spawn 2 clone adds that can be kited and/or rooted until the main boss is dead. Paladins and Rangers work great for this.
– 1.3 Million HP– Hits 3500 (35 Heroic Stamina)– Rampages– Cannot be slowed– Casts Vallon’s Precision (PBAE, Small AC/DoT Debuff, Increase aggro by 40%, Unresistable, 35 sec recast)– Casts Tactical Strike (Single Target, FD Tank + 30 second stun, 24 second recast)
Saryrn
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/Saryrn-Cut.png | alt=
Make sure to cure Torrent of Agony on the main tank with a disease cure. It will lower the tanks aggro and also slow them.
She will spawn 4 adds roughly 90-95%, 50%, and 10%. When looking into the room, the ones that spawn on the right are mezzable. The ones on the left should be tanked or controlled. You can kill them as they spawn or CC them until the end. She has a PBAE Mana Drain as well.
– 1.5 Million HP– Hits 2000+ (35 Heroic Stamina)– Rampages– Cannot be slowed– Casts Torrent of Agony (Single Target, 1500 DD/Decreased aggro 60%/40% Slow, -300 Poison Based, 20 disease counters, 24 sec recast)– Casts Horrifying Affliction (PBAE, lowers spell damage by up to 50%/-500 Mana/-175 Mana Drain DoT, -300 Disease Based, 30 sec recast)
Terris-Thule
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/Terris-Thule-Cut.png | alt=
Tank and spank fight with adds. Similar fight to Saryrn. She will spawn 4 adds roughly 90-95%, 50%, and 10%. When looking into the room, the ones that spawn on the right are mezzable. The ones on the left should be tanked or controlled. You can kill them as they spawn or CC them until the end.
She has a PBAE mana drain and a PBAE increase Hate Aggro debuff as well. So watch aggro!
