Cazic-Thule
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/Cazic-Thule-Cut.png | alt=
He does a Single Target Fear on the tank. Have a Knight with Fear Immunity AA tank for this reason. He has a nasty AE silence and puts out some decent AE damage. Can be rough if you have low resists. Group/AE heals will be needed. He can be pulled out of his room.
– 1.9 Million HP – Hits 1500+ (35 Heroic Stamina) – Cannot be slowed – Rampages – Casts Aura of Fear (PBAE, 2200 DD+600 DoT, -150 Poison Based, lasts 30 seconds, 60 second recast) – Casts Call of the Faceless (PBAE, Silence+Snare, -150 Poison Based, lasts 30 seconds, 90 sec recast)– Casts Timeless Panic (Single Target on tank, Fear + 1000 DD, -350 Magic Based, lasts 6 seconds) **This is listed as PBAE but it seems to just be the single target tank Fear. Only Time I have seen it land is when I was actually tanking Cazic-Thule myself. There is no AE fear on this fight.**
Bertoxxulous
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/Bertoxxulous-Cut.png | alt=
Bertoxxulous starts off highly resistant to spells with moderately low melee damage. Each time you see the emote below his resists weaken and melee damage goes up:Blisters and festering sores covering Bertoxxulous’s hide burst. A noxious ooze bleeds down his scarred flesh, strengthening his physical body.He does this 3 times per fight. His melee damage seems to get stronger the closer to 50% he is, than once past 50% his melee gets weaker again.
After 1st emote 1300+ max hit After 2nd emote 1700+ max hit After 3rd emote 1900+ max hit
Then his melee trickles back down to 1400 again slowly from 50% to 0% hp
He doesn’t leash to his room so you can kill him anywhere on the floor.
– 1.9 Million HP – Hits 1900+ – Rampages – Cannot be slowed – Casts Rain of Bile (Targeted AE, -275 Mana/1100 DD, -300 Poison Based, 60 sec recast)– Casts Black Plague (PBAE, 80% Snare/-800 DoT, -300 Disease Based, 18 Disease counter cure, 60 sec recast)
Rallos Zek
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/Rallos-Zek-Cut.png | alt=
Rallos Zek hits hard, fast, and has high accuracy. He can drop a tank in an instant. Use defensive for this guy. The lower his HP the more damage he will deal out.
His power gains are: 75% – Hits harder and gets AE rampage 50% – Also Flurries 25% – Increased Attack
He will also spawn adds at certain HP%. They aren’t very difficult. Kill them as they spawn. You can wait to kill the last wave after Rallos Zek is dead if you wish.
90% – 4 adds 75% – 4 adds 50% – 4 adds25% – 6 adds
Lastly, every 7 minutes he casts the self buff Blind Rage when out of combat. Make sure to dispel this before or during engage. See spell details below.
– 1.9 Million HP – Hits 2600+ (35 Heroic Stamina) – Rampages – AE Rampages – Flurries – Cannot be slowed – Casts Blind Rage (Self Buff, dispellable, Increases Attack Speed by 65%/+250 ATK/+180AC) – Casts Rage of Zek (PBAE, -400 DoT, -50 Mana Drain/Tick, -100 Fire Based, 9 curse counter, 35 second recast) – Casts Vindicitive Strike (Single Target, 20 second recast, casted on a random raid member, 3850 DD + 20 second stun, -200 Magic Based)
Innoruuk
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/Innoruuk-Cut.png | alt=
Spawns adds during the fight:80% – 3 adds20% – 4 adds
You can kill them as they spawn or CC them until the end of the fight. Paladins rooting them is ideal for CC. Remember to debuff and slow them. He also does a PBAE Fear once per minute. Have a Knight with Fear Immunity AA tank for this reason. Healers can heal through the wall from the “click to Quarm” room to avoid the Fear (see video guide at the top of this page).
– 1.9 Million HP – Hits 2200+ (35 Heroic Stamina) – Rampages – Cannot be slowed– Casts Barrier of Hatred (Self Buff, 225 DS, small damage absorb, 20 sec recast)– Casts Seething Hatred (PBAE, Fear, 75% Aggro Reduction, -200 Fire Based, 60 second recast)
Quarm
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/Quarm-Cut.png | alt=
– 2 Million HP – Hits 4500+ (35 Heroic Stamina)– Light AE Rampage – Slowable
Quarm has a wide variety of spells. Every 25% he will dispel himself (Balance of the Nameless) of a bunch of debuffs and you’ll have to debuff him again (Including Slow!!). Quarm will also lose a head each time this happens.
Quarm also spawns adds a time vortex that need to be mezzed. They will proc Time Warp on anyone they attack. This is a pretty nasty debuff and needs to be disease cured.
Time Warp (Single Target, -50% Slow/-50% Spell Haste, 36 disease counter cure)
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/PoTime_Raid_Guide/a-time-vortex.jpg | alt=
