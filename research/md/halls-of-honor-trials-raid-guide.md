Trial 1 – Trydan Faye
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/Raid_Guides/HoH_Trials/Trydan-Daye-hoh-dragon.jpg | alt=
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/Raid_Guides/HoH_Trials/custodian-of-marr.jpg | alt=
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/Raid_Guides/HoH_Trials/RyddaDar.jpg | alt=
This trial is in the Northeast section of Halls of Honor. It is the “dragon” fight trial.
To begin say “we are ready” to Trydan Faye. Doing so will spawn A Custodian of Marr (trivial). Kill it to spawn Rydda`Dar.
– Hits 2,000+– Rampages– Approx. 500K HP– Casts Crystal Roar (PBAE, Fear/-450 AC/-35% Slow/Snare, -200 Magic Based)– Procs Screeching Bellow (Single Target , 2500 DD/700 DoT, -200 Magic Based)
Once dead, Hail Trydan Faye for your flag. You’ll receive white text:An ethereal mist descends from the air and surrounds your very being. Your body begins to emanate with the power of Trydan Faye.
Trial 2 – Rhaliq Trell
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/Raid_Guides/HoH_Trials/Rhaliq-Trell.jpg | alt=
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/Raid_Guides/HoH_Trials/custodian-of-marr.jpg | alt=
This trial is in the Northwest section of Halls of Honor.
This is a 3 room event. Say “we are ready” to Rhaliq Trell to begin the event. Doing so will spawn A Custodian of Marr (trivial). Kill it and a bunch mobs will spawn in each room.
You can either split your raid evenly into 3 groups or you can put most of the raid in Room 1 (see map) and have kite teams for the other two rooms. The goal is to kill all the mobs in each room while protecting the villagers in Room 1. Several moderately difficult mobs spawn in each room so if your raid is small its best to kite the mobs in Room 2 and Room 3 until Room 1 can clear theirs and come help the other rooms. If you have a big raid you can tank the mobs the traditional way.
Once everything is dead you can Hail Rhaliq Trell for your flag. He will be in Room 1 You’ll receive white text:An ethereal mist descends from the air and surrounds your very being. Your body begins to emanate with the power of Rhaliq Trell.
Trial 3 – Alekson Garn
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/Raid_Guides/HoH_Trials/Alekson-Garn.jpg | alt=
[IMG] https://www.eqprogression.com/wp-content/uploads/Raid_Guides/HoH_Trials/custodian-of-marr.jpg | alt=
This trial is in the Southeast section of Halls of Honor.
It is somewhat similar to Trial 2 as it’s also a 3 room event. Say “we are ready” to Alekson Garn to begin the event. Doing so will spawn A Custodian of Marr (trivial). Kill it and a bunch mobs will spawn in each room.
Split your raid into 3 even groups. Roughly 5 – 6 mobs spawn in each room. After they die a named spawn will spawn in each room. Once all 3 named are dead you win the event.
It’s recommended to have at least couple tanks and healers in each room along with a slower. Two Vex Thal geared groups per room is very doable. Rooms can help each other once theirs is clear.
Named Info:Room 1: Advocent Joran – Hits 1,000+, FlurriesRoom 2: Halgoz Rellinic – Hits 1,000+, RampagesRoom 3: Freegan Haun – Hits 1,000+, Rampages
Once complete, you can Hail Alekson Garn for your flag. He will be in Room 1. You’ll receive white text:An ethereal mist descends from the air and surrounds your very being. Your body begins to emanate with the power of Alekson Garn.
